Sri Lanka

The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Introduction

Sri Lanka, historically known as Ceylon, is an island nation located in South Asia, right in the heart of the Indian Ocean. Renowned for its rich cultural heritage, diverse landscapes, and warm hospitality, this tropical paradise has a documented history that spans over 3,000 years.

Key Facts

  • Legislative Capital: Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte
  • Commercial Capital: Colombo
  • Official Languages: Sinhala and Tamil
  • Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)

Top Tourist Attractions

Despite its small size, Sri Lanka offers an incredible variety of travel experiences:

  1. Sigiriya Rock Fortress: An ancient palace complex and a UNESCO World Heritage site, famous for its frescoes and engineering marvels.
  2. The Cultural Triangle: Home to ancient cities like Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa, showcasing magnificent stupas and ruins.
  3. The Central Highlands: Known for the scenic town of Ella, rolling lush green tea plantations, and the iconic Nine Arch Bridge.
  4. Wildlife Parks: Destinations like Yala National Park, which features one of the highest leopard densities in the world.

Culture and Cuisine

Sri Lankan culture is deeply influenced by Buddhism and Hinduism. The country is also world-famous for its spices, especially Ceylon Cinnamon, and its delicious culinary staple: Rice and Curry, often accompanied by a variety of colorful side dishes (sambols).
